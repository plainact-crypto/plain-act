## Step 2 implementation decisions

1. Placeholder surfaces are not allowed to remain both prominent and indexable.
2. `Coming Soon`, `DO THE WORK`, and the thin Quotes collection are now `noindex,follow`.
3. `Coming Soon` is removed from the global primary navigation and footer.
4. All NEXORA routes have been removed from `src/pages`, so they are no longer generated as Plain Act public routes.
5. The generated sitemap already excludes `404`, rendered `noindex` pages, and explicitly excluded unrelated prefixes. After this change, the sitemap should shrink from the prior 32 indexable URLs because three formerly indexable low-value routes are removed from the index surface.
6. Article and book-overlap quality judgments that require content rewriting remain explicitly scheduled for Step 4 rather than being silently treated as complete here.
